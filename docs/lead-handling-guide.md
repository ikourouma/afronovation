# Afronovation: Handling Website Leads

A guide for the Afronovation team. It explains what happens when someone fills in a form on afronovation.com, and what to do next.

---

## 1. Where leads arrive

Every form submission is saved in two places:

1. **The admin dashboard:** sign in at `afronovation.com/admin/login` and open **Leads**. The dashboard shows how many new leads are waiting.
2. **An email alert to hello@afronovation.com** with the person's details. Reply directly to that email to answer them.

Nothing is lost if the email alert fails: the admin **Leads** page is the master list.

## 2. Lead types and what to do

Each lead shows a label in blue at the top of its card.

| Label in Leads | Where it came from | Respond within | First action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Executive briefing** | "Book a Briefing" buttons | 1 business day | Confirm interest and propose 2-3 meeting times for a 30-minute call. |
| **Investor brief** | "Get the investor brief" | 1 business day | Send a short note with the Capabilities Portfolio and offer a call with leadership. |
| **Partnership** | "Partner with us" | 2 business days | Ask what they deliver and in which countries; route to the partnerships lead. |
| **Consultant network** | "Join the network" | 5 business days | Thank them, ask for a CV or LinkedIn profile and their availability; add to the talent list. |
| **Platform demo** | "Request a demo" on a platform page (the platform name is shown) | 1 business day | Confirm the use case and book a demo with the platform owner. |
| **Download: first-visible-service-roadmap** | The roadmap form on Insights | 2-3 business days | Send the *download follow-up* email below. |
| **Download: capabilities-portfolio-2026** | The portfolio form on About or Insights | 2-3 business days | Send the *download follow-up* email below. |
| **General enquiry** | The contact page without a specific button | 1 business day | Answer the question; offer a briefing if relevant. |

**Prioritise** ministries, agencies, embassies, development partners and investors. The organisation name is on each lead.

## 3. Keep the status up to date

Change the status on each lead card as you work:

- **New:** nobody has replied yet.
- **Contacted:** we have replied and are waiting for an answer.
- **Qualified:** a real opportunity (meeting booked, proposal requested).
- **Closed:** finished: converted, not a fit, or no reply after two follow-ups.

**Every Monday:** review all leads still marked *New* or *Contacted*. Use **Export CSV** if you need a spreadsheet for reporting.

## 4. Email templates

Adapt the wording, but keep it short and personal.

**Download follow-up (roadmap or portfolio)**

> Subject: Your First Visible Service roadmap
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for downloading our [roadmap / Capabilities Portfolio]. If you are weighing which service to digitise first, we would be glad to run a 30-minute working session with your team, with no obligation.
>
> Would [two dates] suit you? Or book directly: afronovation.com/contact?intent=briefing
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name], Afronovation

**Briefing confirmation**

> Subject: Your executive briefing with Afronovation
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for your request. We have reserved [date and time, time zone] for a 30-minute briefing. To make it useful, could you share your top two priorities and who will join from your side?

> [Your name], Afronovation

**Consultant network**

> Subject: Joining the Afronovation consultant network
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for your interest in working with us. Please reply with your CV or LinkedIn profile, your areas of expertise and your availability. We will be in touch when a matching engagement opens.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name], Afronovation

## 5. Newsletter subscribers

- People who tick "send me occasional insights" (in the footer or on a download form) appear under **Subscribers**.
- They stay **Pending** until they click the confirmation link we email them. **Only email people marked Confirmed.**
- Use **Export CSV** to load confirmed subscribers into your email tool. Every newsletter must include an unsubscribe link.

## 6. Privacy and data requests

People agree to be contacted when they submit a form; that consent is recorded. Treat their details as confidential:

- Do not forward lead lists outside the team or add people to other lists without their consent.
- **Unsubscribe request:** open **Subscribers** and click **Remove** next to their email (Platform Admin).
- **"Delete my data" request:** remove them from **Subscribers** and click **Delete lead** on each of their leads (Platform Admin). Also delete the related alert emails from the inbox. Reply to confirm within 30 days.
- Every removal is recorded in the **Change log**.

## 7. Spam and junk

The forms block most automated spam. If junk still gets through, mark it **Closed**; a Platform Admin can delete it.

## 8. Who can see leads

Anyone with an admin account (Platform Admins and Editors) can view and update leads. Only Platform Admins can delete records or manage accounts. Accounts are created by the Platform Admin under **Users**.
