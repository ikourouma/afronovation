Step 2 · Deliver in 90 days

From selection to a live service

Days 1–15
Align
  • Name the executive sponsor and delivery lead
  • Agree success measures
  • Record today's baseline
Days 16–45
Map and configure
  • Map the journey with the people who run it
  • Remove duplicate steps
  • Configure on shared foundations
Days 46–75
Pilot
  • One agency, real users
  • Train staff and assisted channels
  • Fix friction weekly
Days 76–90
Go live and report
  • Launch with support
  • Publish the results
  • Choose the next services

Change management, training and knowledge transfer run through every phase, so your team can operate and extend the service.

Measure what wins the next budget

Processing timeDays from application to decision, before and after.
Cost per transactionStaff time, paper and visits saved per application.
AdoptionShare of applications completed online or with assistance.
Revenue collectedFees received on time, reconciled automatically.
Citizen satisfactionA short rating after every completed service.

Governance checklist

  • An executive sponsor who unblocks decisions
  • One accountable delivery lead and team
  • Data protection and national data sovereignty agreed in writing
  • A training and knowledge-transfer plan for local staff
  • Continuity arrangements agreed at the outset
  • A public results report at day 90

After day 90: scale with confidence

Each new service becomes a configuration, not a new procurement. Our 365-day National Digital Acceleration Program extends it across ministries.
